Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Short version: TraceOff removes metadata from your files entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded anywhere, we don't run any servers that see your media, and we don't collect personal data.

1. What TraceOff does with your files

TraceOff reads and edits image, video, audio, PDF and Word, Excel and PowerPoint files that you choose to open in the app — through the Photos picker, Files, the share sheet, paste, drag and drop, or Shortcuts. All processing happens locally on your device using Apple's system frameworks. Your photos and videos are never transmitted to us, uploaded to a server, or shared with any third party as part of using the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your photos, videos, or any metadata extracted from them
  • Your name, email address, or contact information (unless you email us directly for support)
  • Your location, including the location stored in your files and, if you use "Use My Location", where your device is. Both stay on your device (see section 4)
  • Contacts or other personal data on your device
  • An account or profile of any kind — TraceOff does not require sign-in

TraceOff does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.

3. In-app purchases

TraceOff Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time purchase through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. Apple may share limited, non-identifying transaction information with us (such as purchase status) solely to unlock Pro features on your device. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles this data.

4. Permissions

Photos. TraceOff may request access to your Photos library to let you pick and save photos and videos, to replace an original with its cleaned copy when you ask it to, and to run the optional library audit. The audit reads the metadata of items in your library on your device to count how many carry a location or an AI label. Nothing from your library is uploaded, and the results never leave your device.

iCloud Photos. If a photo or video you pick is stored in iCloud Photos and its original is not on your device yet, iOS downloads that original from Apple so TraceOff can read it. The download is made by Apple's Photos framework, under Apple's Privacy Policy; nothing is sent to us.

Replacing an original. "Save & Delete Original" saves the cleaned copy to your library and then asks iOS to delete the original, which iOS asks you to confirm. The original goes to Recently Deleted in Photos, where it stays for 30 days.

Clipboard. TraceOff reads the clipboard only when you paste into it or run Clean Clipboard, and writes to it only when you copy a cleaned file.

Location. TraceOff asks for your device's location only if you tap "Use My Location" in the metadata editor, to write that position into the file you are editing. The permission is "while using the app", the position is read once, and it is not stored or sent to us.

Maps. When you search for a place or view or pick a location on a map, the place name or coordinates involved are sent to Apple's mapping services to return map tiles, search results and place names. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us, and we do not receive it.

iCloud settings sync. If you switch on "Sync settings with iCloud" (off by default), your saved presets, private places (the name, coordinates and radius of each place you protect), file-naming choices and the saved creator and copyright are stored in your iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your other devices use the same settings. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. No photo, video, document or report is ever synced. Switching sync off stops further syncing; the values already in iCloud stay there until you remove them from your iCloud account.

5. Data retention

Nothing you process leaves your device, so there is no server-side retention. On your device, TraceOff keeps your recent batches — a copy of each file and its cleaned copy, with the findings report — in the app's own private storage, so that you can still save or share them later. Batches with cleaned copies that have not been saved to Photos or Files are kept until you save or explicitly delete them. Other batches are limited to the 20 most recent and are removed after 30 days or as soon as you delete them in the app. Your settings, presets and the day's free-clean count are also stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it, except for settings you chose to sync with iCloud.

6. Children's privacy

TraceOff is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.
